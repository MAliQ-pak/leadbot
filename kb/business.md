# BrightPath Solar (sample business for demo)

## About
BrightPath Solar installs residential and commercial solar systems in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. We have been operating since 2019 and have completed over 800 installations.

## Services
We offer rooftop solar panel installation, hybrid inverter setup, lithium battery backup, net metering paperwork, and yearly maintenance plans.

## Pricing
A 5 kW on-grid system starts from about Rs 650,000. A 10 kW hybrid system with battery backup starts from about Rs 1,900,000. Final price depends on roof size, panel brand, and battery choice. We give a free written quote after a site visit.

## Packages
We offer three packages: a 5 kW on-grid system starting from about Rs 650,000, a 10 kW hybrid system with battery backup starting from about Rs 1,900,000, and a yearly maintenance plan for homes at Rs 15,000 per year. Final price depends on roof size, panel brand, and battery choice. We give a free written quote after a site visit.

## Installation time
Most home installations take 3 to 5 days after the site visit is approved. Net metering approval usually takes 4 to 8 weeks and we handle the paperwork for you.

## Warranty
Solar panels come with a 12 year product warranty and a 25 year performance warranty. Inverters carry a 5 year warranty. Batteries carry a 5 to 10 year warranty depending on brand.

## Financing
We offer 6, 12, and 24 month installment plans through partner banks. A 20 percent down payment is required for installments.

## Site visit
Site visits are free in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Book by sharing your name, phone number, and address.

## Working hours and contact
Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 7 pm. A human sales advisor will call back within one working day.

## Maintenance
Yearly maintenance includes panel cleaning, inverter check, wiring inspection, and a performance report. The plan costs Rs 15,000 per year for homes.
