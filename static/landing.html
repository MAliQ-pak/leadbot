How it works

From sign-up to your website in four steps

  1. Sign up and pick a templateSolar installer, shoe store, or start from scratch. Your bot works straight away.
  2. Teach it your businessEdit the text, import your website, upload a price list, or add questions and answers.
  3. Test it liveChat with your bot in the preview while you set it up. Change the name, colour and greeting.
  4. Paste one lineCopy the embed code into your website. Leads arrive on your own dashboard.

What you get

Everything a small sales team needs

Answers only from your text

It never invents a price or a policy. If it is not sure, it says so and offers a callback.

English and Roman Urdu

Replies in the language and script the customer uses, with Pakistani spellings understood.

Every lead scored

Hot, warm or cold with the reason, from explainable rules plus a trained classifier.

Your own pipeline

Drag deals through stages named for your industry, with notes and the full chat.

Learns from what it missed

Questions it could not answer wait for you. Answer once and it knows from then on.

Never goes silent

If the AI is busy or offline, a built-in fallback still answers from your text.
